Before you begin

Set your phone to do not disturb and grab your pen and journal. Get comfortable and take a deep breath.

Bring to mind whatever you most want clarity on today. It doesn't need to be fully formed. A feeling, a naggingly vague situation, or a question half-asked is enough.

Open your journal to a fresh page. Write your question at the top . Whatever situation or area of life you want more clarity on.

Then tap Begin and shuffle the cards. Stop whenever it feels right.
